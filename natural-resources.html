The Agency Path

Organize. Unify. Scale.

The same three steps we use with every agency, shaped around how a resource agency actually works.

01
Organize

Scan and sort what you have to keep.

We work from your agency's records retention schedule, so permanent records get scanned and indexed, and the rest stops taking up space.

  • Pickup with GPS-tracked chain of custody, start to finish
  • Records indexed the way your staff search, like permit, lease, or parcel number
  • Large-format engineering drawings and maps scanned and indexed for search
  • Originals returned, stored, or securely destroyed, your call

Unify

Move it all into a system you already own.

Records from paper and older systems go into Microsoft 365 and SharePoint, with their structure, retention, and permissions intact. Rather have us run it? 1DocStop is our hosted option.

  • Digital-to-digital migration out of older records software
  • Records linked to locations on your agency's ArcGIS maps
  • OneForm turns field and permit forms into online forms with approvals
