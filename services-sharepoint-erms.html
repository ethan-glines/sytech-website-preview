Scenarios

Built Inside the License They Had.

We already pay for our Microsoft licenses. Why would we fund a second records platform?

One county reached the same conclusion under budget cuts. Their existing software had been built for outdated, infrequent tasks and could not adapt, but paying for new software on top of already-expensive licenses was unjustifiable — and the county-specific low-code platform they actually wanted looked out of reach. We digitized their documents and built low-code solutions inside the Microsoft products they already owned, open and scalable enough to add capabilities like APIs over time. Watch the county explain how they did more with less of their budget.

Can one system inside our own tenant track projects and their funding requirements?

It can. A county told us that as its IT projects multiplied, each with funding requirements that needed meticulous tracking across the life cycle, it needed a standardized approach and had nowhere central to run it. They had the process defined — PAL, the Project Application Life Cycle — and no application to manage it in. We built low-code solutions inside their existing Microsoft products, and each project is now overseen in correspondence with PAL. Watch the county describe the result.
