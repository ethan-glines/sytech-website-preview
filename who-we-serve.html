In Their Own Words

Common Scenarios.

Our volunteer applications are scattered across forms, attachments, and links that break. Can that be fixed?

Yes. A district came to us struggling to onboard volunteers for its campuses and district office: the forms and the electronic attachments tied to each application were disorganized, and the links sent to volunteers frequently did not work. We built the application, the attachments, and the electronic signatures into the Microsoft apps the district already owned, so everything lands in one secure, cloud-based place. The district says it saw an increase in volunteers once the process got easy. Watch them tell it.

Paper purchase orders are burying us and vendor payments get missed. What does the digital version look like?

It looks like a notification instead of a stack of paper. A public agency came to us after a surge in demand for services left it managing physical purchase orders by hand, with payments to local contractors and businesses slipping through the cracks. We digitized their existing documents and built the approval path inside the Microsoft environment they already owned: department heads are notified when an invoice is received, and they approve it digitally before Accounts Payable generates a payment. Watch the agency walk through the change.

Our county has more IT projects than we can track, each with its own funding requirements. Where do we start?

With a standard process, and one place to run it from. A county told us it had numerous projects underway, each with funding requirements that needed meticulous tracking across the whole life cycle, and no central application to manage the work or report on it. They had a structured process in mind — PAL, the Project Application Life Cycle. We built low-code solutions inside their existing Microsoft products so every project is now overseen in correspondence with PAL. Watch the county describe the approach they landed on.

The Two Personas

We Meet You Where You Are.

Most public agencies fall into one of two camps when they come to SyTech. We work with both — and the path forward looks different for each.

Persona 1

Still Using Paper

Paper forms still drive many of your business processes. Forms move slowly. Approvals take time. Staff re-enter data into different systems. Records are hard to track and harder to audit.

You know modernization is overdue, but you are wary of adding another piece of software to manage. SyTech meets you here — we digitize, structure, and migrate your records into the cloud platform you already own. No new license. No second login.

Persona 2

Already Using E-Forms Software

You have adopted electronic forms tools, but they are costly and disconnected. Your current platform sits outside your records system, creates silos, and does not connect to the workflows you actually need.

You are looking for a better path that leverages the cloud investments you have already made. SyTech replaces standalone tools with a unified ecosystem inside your existing Microsoft 365 or Google environment — fewer vendors, lower costs, more control.
